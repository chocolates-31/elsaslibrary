01 · The Author of This Library

A Little About Me

I'm Elsa, a Translation and Interpreting student at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. I'm interested in everything that happens when words travel from one language to another: the nuances that shift, the cultural references that need context, and the ideas that deserve to be understood beyond linguistic borders.

To me, translation is about much more than finding equivalents between words. It also involves research, interpreting intentions, recognising cultural differences and making responsible decisions. Sometimes, the most important work happens precisely in what is left unsaid.

I'm still building my professional path. This library is, in part, a record of that journey: what I learn, the projects I develop and the questions that help me keep growing.

09 · Between the Lines

How I Approach Translation

I believe in the value of carefully chosen words, but also in all the work behind them: consulting reliable sources, checking terminology, revising the text and understanding its intended audience.

I want to build a professional practice based on curiosity, accuracy, cultural awareness and a willingness to learn. I know every assignment has its own needs, and a good linguistic solution cannot come from applying the same formula to every text.

Translation builds a bridge between what someone wants to express and what another person needs to understand.
